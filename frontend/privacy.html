Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

LoanNiti ("we", "us") provides a free EMI calculator, loan prepayment planner, and related tools at loanniti-eta.vercel.app and in the LoanNiti Android app. This page explains what data we collect, why, and how you can control it.

The short version

  • Your loan numbers (amount, rate, tenure, etc.) are calculated entirely in your browser or device and are not sent to our servers unless you use the AI chat assistant.
  • We don't require an account, and we never ask for bank account numbers, card details, or government ID.
  • We collect an email address only if you choose to subscribe for updates.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Email addressIf you subscribe for updatesTo send you occasional product updates. You can ask us to delete it at any time.
Chat messagesIf you use the AI loan assistantSent to our AI provider (Groq) to generate a reply. Used only to answer your question; not used to build an advertising profile.
IP address & basic request logsEvery visit/requestRate-limiting, abuse prevention, and debugging. Kept only as long as needed for these purposes.
Usage analytics (pages viewed, approximate location, device type)Full analytics only if you accept cookiesCollected via Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set and Google receives only limited signals without identifiers. Loan numbers are removed from page addresses before anything is sent.

Third parties we use

  • Google Analytics — anonymised usage analytics. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Groq — processes AI chat messages to generate responses. See Groq's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel — hosts the website.
  • Railway — hosts our backend server and the database that stores subscriber email addresses.

We do not sell your data to anyone.

Your choices

  • You can use the calculators without giving us any personal information at all.
  • You can accept or decline analytics cookies, and change your choice any time in cookie settings.
  • To unsubscribe or delete your email from our list, email us (below) and we'll remove it.
  • You can clear your browser's local storage at any time to remove any saved scenarios, which are stored only on your own device.

Children's privacy

LoanNiti is a financial calculator intended for adults managing loans and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
